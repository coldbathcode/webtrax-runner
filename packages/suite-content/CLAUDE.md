# @webtrax/suite-content

Content (C06.5, first slice; `plan/06-check-suites/seo-a11y-content.md` §3), suite id `content`, rule ids `content.*`. HTTP only, deterministic, on by default in the CLI and hosted scans. No dictionary, no AI.

- `visibleText($, main?)`: the text people read, blocks on their own lines. Never reads `<code>`, `<pre>`, `<kbd>`, `<samp>`, scripts, styles, templates or form controls; with `main`, also leaves out nav, header, footer, aside and forms (uses `<main>` when the page has one).
- `onPage`: `content.placeholder` (high; one finding per kind per page, `evidence_key` the kind: `lorem_ipsum`, `editor_prompt`, `insert_marker`, `template_tag`, `empty_value`; the evidence quotes the text). `content.empty_page` (medium) when the main text and image alt text have fewer than 20 words; not for a meta-refresh stub, a noindex page or a page with a form (sign in, search, contact).
- `onSite`: `content.copyright_stale` (low, site-wide, one finding: the newest year any notice names is behind this year; `config.today` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) pins the date for tests). `content.duplicate` (low): pages of 80 words or more whose main text has a SimHash within 3 of 64 bits and a similar length, grouped in URL order, one finding per group on its first URL; a page whose canonical names another URL, or a noindex page, is left out.
- **Precision first.** A sentence that mentions "lorem ipsum" or "Click here to edit" is not filler: filler is the classic opening or a block that starts with it, and an editor prompt is a short block of its own. `undefined`, `NaN` and `null` count only as a whole block or beside a label, currency or unit. Before loosening a pattern, add the prose it must not match to the "leaves ordinary prose alone" test. A lone `undefined`, `NaN` or `null` is left out on a page about code (3 or more code samples, or one of those words in its title or headings). A copyright notice is a line of 120 characters or fewer. Found on the first real runs: the suite's own help text quoting a notice on its docs page, webtrax.dev's own feature list mentions lorem ipsum, MDN's NaN page is headed "NaN", and a sign-in page has few words.
- Not yet: `content.date_stale`, `content.dictionary` (needs suite config in `webtrax.yml`), `content.spelling`, `content.readability`, `content.mixed_language`, `content.embed_broken` (browser), and the AI rules (after GA, D6).
